## What this can and cannot show

- A run is one reading per contract at temperature 0; repeat noise is unmeasured.
- The three variants of a planted family share code, so the 12 originals are the independent items; the renamed copies are a control, not extra evidence.
- Intervals resample items, so with 12 items a 95% interval is about ±25 points wide either side near 50%.
- On the planted originals the arms disagree on 0 of 12 items (A only 0, P only 0, sign test p = 1.0). 0 disagreements are too few for any split to be significant.
- Incident results may be memorised and have 5 items at most: read them as anecdotes.
- Draft labels are unreviewed; a flagged function that is not labelled counts as a false positive even when it may be a real second issue.
